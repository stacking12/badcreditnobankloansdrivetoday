Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what most buyers ask before visiting our Jersey City lot.

What is Buy Here Pay Here financing and how does it work?

Buy Here Pay Here means the dealership finances your car directly, instead of sending you to a bank or outside lender. You choose your car, get approved on-site, and make your payments right here — which is why approval is faster and less dependent on a perfect credit score.

Can I get approved with bad credit or no credit history?

Yes. Our in-house financing was built for exactly this situation. We look at your ability to make payments today, not just a credit score, so bad credit, thin credit files, and past repossessions don't automatically disqualify you.

What do I need to bring to drive today?

Generally you'll need a valid driver's license, proof of income, and proof of residence. Bring these with you and our team can usually complete approval and paperwork the same visit, so you can drive today.

Do you accept trade-ins or require a down payment?

We accept trade-ins toward your purchase, and down payment requirements are set to stay flexible and affordable based on the vehicle and your situation — ask our team for exact terms on the car you're interested in.

Is there a Spanish-speaking representative available?

Yes, we have Spanish-speaking staff available to help explain financing terms, walk through paperwork, and answer questions in Spanish throughout the buying process.
